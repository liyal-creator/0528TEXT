Eight Main-Character Moments

Episode 1

The hype machine overheats

He paid $4.568M for the Buffett lunch, hyped it heavily, postponed it, then publicly admitted to “over-marketing.”
What happened
In 2019 he won Buffett's charity lunch for $4,567,888. After the original plan was postponed, Sun apologized for excessive promotion; the meeting finally happened in 2020.
Pro take
Was $4M+ expensive — or an unusually effective way to buy weeks of global financial-media attention?
Skeptical take
So was this a charity lunch — or one giant publicity stunt?
CT being CT
The impressive part wasn't buying the lunch. It was turning one meal into a whole season of content.
Episode 2

Crypto founder → “His Excellency”

From crypto founder to diplomatic title — the character arc suddenly jumped genres.
Why it's funny
On profiles and at public events, “Justin Sun” increasingly became “H.E. Justin Sun.” To the internet, this looked less like a normal career move and more like crypto suddenly unlocking a diplomacy expansion pack.
